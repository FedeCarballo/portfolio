Questions about SEO and web development

What clients usually ask before we start.

Send a question

What does a technical SEO audit include?

I review the HTML Google receives, metadata, canonicals, sitemaps, structured data and Core Web Vitals. You get every issue with its evidence and a plan ranked by impact.

Do you work with VTEX IO stores?

Yes, since 2023: store builds, migrations from VTEX Legacy, SSR on listing and product pages, sitemaps and JSON-LD.

What is GEO, or SEO for AI search?

Preparing your site so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews understand it and cite it.

Do you work with clients outside Argentina?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Latin America and beyond, in English or Spanish.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes show up in indexing within weeks; rankings usually follow in three to six months. We track it in Search Console.
